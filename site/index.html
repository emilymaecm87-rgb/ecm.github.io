I am a second-year PhD student in Health Policy and Management at the Yale School of Public Health. My current research examines how policies such as immigration enforcement impact the long-term care workforce and shapes access to and quality of care for aging adults. My prospective research seeks to evaluate the impact of historical federal funding withdrawals on state Medicaid Home and Community-Based Services coverage and beneficiaries clinical outcomes.

Before Yale I spent about three years doing applied policy research in Hong Kong, evaluating a large-scale poverty alleviation trial across fifteen transitional housing sites and co-authoring reports on health financing, primary care, and community mental health. I hold a Master of Public Health and a BA in Psychology from Brown University.
